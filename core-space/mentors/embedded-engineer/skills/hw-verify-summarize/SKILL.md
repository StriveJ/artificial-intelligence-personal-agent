## Trigger phrases
- verification summary / test summary / hardware verification

## Boundaries
- Summarize completed evidence; do not rerun verification or execute tests.
- Design briefs belong to hw-design-brief-draft.
- Missing evidence must be marked unverified/pending; never invent conclusions.

## Action sequence
1. Collect verification items and pass/fail results.
2. Identify blockers and residual risks.
3. State conclusions and next steps.

## Dependencies
- None.

## Verification
- Include passed items, failed items, open issues, and conclusions.
